🎯 What Is This?

Amnezia VPN Panel is a user-friendly web dashboard that makes managing your VPN server incredibly easy. Think of it as a control center for your VPN - you can see who's connected, monitor traffic, and manage your clients all from one beautiful interface. No complicated command lines, no technical knowledge needed.

Perfect for: Home users, small business owners, anyone who wants privacy and control over their VPN without the headache of complex server management.

🚀 Features That Make Life Easier

  • 🔑 Simple Login Built-in login form directly in the web panel - secure access right from your browser.
  • 👥 Client Management View and manage all connected clients in one clean list. Add, remove, or modify clients with a click.
  • 📊 Real-Time Traffic Watch live bandwidth usage and data consumption for each client - know exactly what's happening on your VPN.
  • 📱 QR Codes & Configs Generate QR codes for mobile setup or download .conf files for desktop - get connected in seconds.
  • 🔒 Protocol Support Works with both AmneziaWG and AmneziaWG 3.0 (nginx + Node.js) protocols - flexible and future-proof.
  • 💻 Self-Hosted Full control over your data. Host on your own Ubuntu or Debian server - no third-party services required.

📋 System Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10, Windows 11, or any modern Linux distribution (Ubuntu, Debian)
  • Server: Ubuntu 20.04+ or Debian 11+ with 1GB RAM minimum (2GB recommended)
  • Node.js: Version 16 or higher
  • Nginx: For AmneziaWG 3.0 protocol support
  • Browser: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari (latest versions)

🛠️ How to Use It

Using Amnezia VPN Panel is as simple as using any website. Here's what your first day looks like:

  1. Add Your First Client: Click "Clients" in the menu, then "Add Client". Give it a name (like "My Phone" or "John's Laptop").
  2. Connect a Device: A QR code appears instantly. Open the AmneziaVPN app on your phone or computer, scan the code, and you're connected securely.
  3. Monitor Activity: The "Stats" tab shows live download/upload speeds. You can watch in real-time as your devices communicate.
  4. Remove Unused Clients: Don't recognize a device? Simply click the trash icon next to that client to disconnect and remove it.

🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • Panel won't start? Ensure port 51821 is free. Open your firewall settings and allow TCP port 51821.
  • QR code not working? Update your AmneziaVPN client app to the latest version from your app store.
  • Slow connection? Check the "Traffic" tab to see if multiple clients are downloading large files simultaneously. The panel helps you spot bandwidth hogs quickly.
  • Forgot admin password? Navigate to your installation folder and run the "reset-admin-password" script provided in the download.

🔒 Security Best Practices

Important: Keep your server secure with these simple habits:

  • Always use the latest version of Amnezia VPN Panel (check the Releases page monthly)
  • Enable HTTPS by following the "Setup HTTPS" guide in your installation package
  • Create strong, unique passwords for both your VPN panel and your server's admin account
  • Regularly review your client list and remove any devices you don't recognize
  • Don't share your VPN server's public IP address on social media

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to be a developer to use this?
No! The entire interface is visual. If you can use online banking, you can manage your VPN with this panel.

Q: Can I host it on a Raspberry Pi?
Yes, as long as you run Ubuntu or Debian on it. A Pi 4 with 2GB RAM works well for 5-10 clients.

Q: Is this completely free?
Absolutely. It's open-source software under the MIT license, which means free to use, modify, and distribute.

Q: What happens if the internet on my server goes down?
Your VPN stops working, but the panel stays intact. Once your internet is back, everything runs as before - no data loss.
